CSV batch classification

Import a UTF-8 CSV with age and either httkv_ml_per_m or both tkv_ml and height_m. Optional: case_id, morphology (typical/atypical). Export includes classification and modeled growth. Maximum 1 MB / 5,000 rows. No files are sent to a server.

For research and educational use; not a diagnostic device. No login, tracking, API, or remote processing. Avoid entering directly identifying patient information.

Basis: Irazabal et al., JASN 2015 and KDIGO 2025 ADPKD guideline. This is an independent educational implementation, not the official Mayo calculator.
